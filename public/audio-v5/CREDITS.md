## Spain — Marcha Real

The 97-note melody line is extracted from track1, beats0–64 of JoseanE's explicitly freely released arrangement based on the1761 melody. Only the melody is retained, not its harmony, bass, drum parts, instrument patches or audio. Note durations are quantized to quarter-beat notation and played at120BPM.

## Argentina — Himno Nacional Argentino

Historical melody by Blas Parera. The68-note chorus excerpt is taken from the monophonic VOZ (Trompa) track5, beats232–296, in the reference MIDI linked by Argentina's embassy. The melody is retained; piano accompaniment, string doublings, performance velocities, original tempo changes and audio recordings are not used. Durations are quantized to quarter-beat notation. This references the historical composition, not a claim that every modern edition or recording is freely licensed.

## New arrangement and recording

The toy-style lead, high mallet accents, pop bass/chord rhythm, kick/clap/hi-hats and stereo echoes are newly synthesized for this project, with no vocal generation or sampled master recordings. Both auditions are32seconds,120BPM,16bars, stereo24kHz/16-bit PCM. `melodies.json` preserves sourced note data; `manifest.json` records complete arranged melody events, measured peak/RMS and output SHA256. Generator: `scripts/generate-national-victory.py`. Listening-board MP3s are128kbps encodings of these WAVs. Do not confuse technical playback/metrics verification with subjective human-listening approval.
