# Victory audio v3 credits

- `theme-0.wav`, `theme-1.wav`, `theme-2.wav`: original instrumental loops generated by `scripts/generate-victory-audio.py` using Python's standard-library math and WAV support. No external samples or commercial melodies are used.
- `cheer-yeah.wav`: Kenney Voiceover Pack `Male/you_win.ogg`, recorded by Jeffrey M. Smith; the spoken words are “You win!”.
- `cheer-we-did-it.wav`: Kenney Voiceover Pack `Female/congratulations.ogg`, recorded by Giselle; the spoken words are “Congratulations!”.
- `cheer-champions.wav`: Kenney Voiceover Pack `Female/you_win.ogg`, recorded by Giselle; the spoken words are “You win!”.

The three legacy cheer filenames are retained for compatibility; they do not describe the spoken words. Source recordings are CC0 under the Kenney Voiceover Pack license (`https://kenney.nl/assets/voiceover-pack`, `https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/`). Original pack credits identify the performers. Voice WAVs are direct source conversions to mono 24 kHz PCM; no pitch shifting, EQ, synthesis, cloning, or phrase relabeling. Source archives remain in ignored `.local-server/audio-sources` and are not shipped. `manifest.json` records measured duration, peak/RMS, byte size, and SHA-256 for each bundled v3 WAV. Recreate voice conversions and metrics with `python scripts/prepare-human-shouts.py`; regenerate only instrumental loops with `python scripts/generate-victory-audio.py`.
