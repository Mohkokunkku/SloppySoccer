# Graphical local lobby and bindings

Enabled players have colored keyboard/controller tokens under Spain or Argentina. Drag a token to the other team, click it, or focus the native button and press Enter/Space to switch sides. Each operation updates the existing team select and normalized seat callback; no second assignment store is maintained. The original four enable/team/device controls retain their stable IDs. Duplicate devices are excluded, disconnected/unmapped controllers are reported, and match locking blocks clicks and drops.

Original external SVG diagrams show actual keycaps and a PlayStation-shaped standard controller: WASD/arrows, Space/Enter hold-release shots, E/slash pass, Q/period switch, left/right Shift sprint; left stick, Cross/A, Circle/B, L1/LB, R2/RT and Options/Menu. All three diagrams are available before a controller joins. Each image has a title and textual alternative, plus a short readable binding legend.

The diagrams are in a native disclosure below the pitch. They start expanded for setup and collapse when a match starts, leaving kick meters and gameplay controls visible. They can be reopened during play. The token layout and diagrams have desktop/390px browser coverage, including native keyboard activation, drag/drop, image loading and overflow checks. Unit doubles cover placement, callbacks, locking, duplicates and device status; they are not presented as browser-rendering proof.

Standard mapping is required. Simulated pad tests and diagrams do not prove a physical PlayStation/Xbox controller or its OS pairing worked. See `party2-verification.md` for acceptance results.
